# A page being put together

## Direction — 2026-11-09

The portfolio is an open layout canvas. Three simulated cursors arrange real
content: the author's name, moving Blender studies, and questions from published
notes. Construction is a short reenactment of an existing page, never a claim of
live collaboration, online visitors, or AI generating the author's work.

Palette: canvas #f4f5f8, surface #ffffff, ink #20232b, muted #646c7b,
selection blue #2457ff, secondary cursor coral #c35444. A dark canvas is optional.
Bricolage Grotesque supplies the large signature and counts; Noto Sans SC supplies
Chinese text and controls. English is limited to the author's signature and
familiar tool/file names.

Layout: a narrow document toolbar, broad left-aligned canvas, generous gaps,
oversized name opposite a real moving study, and an understated layer rail.
The hero has one selected media object; lower sections use different proportions,
not a wall of identical cards. Borders and dimension labels denote construction
or selection rather than decorating every paragraph.

Sequence: signature + moving work → real counts and study tags → filterable
Blender studies → questions and complete article archive → short profile/contact.
Project details have image/method/source layers. The complete comic keyboard
remains accessible as a separate mode, without its full-screen canvas blocking
the new layout.

Motion: GSAP types visible text, places media along short cursor paths, and draws
selection frames. Scrolling activates the next section without pinning or forced
scrolling. Controls pause, skip, and replay; Escape skips. Keyboard focus or
interaction completes a section. Reduced motion renders the final page. The
bottom progress reports completed construction objects, not download progress.
Only one Lenis instance exists in each mode. Hidden/offscreen animation pauses.

Assets: six existing local Blender films and their posters; public article
archive, images, formulas and links remain unchanged. No reference-site assets,
identity, metrics, source code, or claimed skills are copied. Native animation
and display-camera footage retain their existing distinctions.

## Reference research

Read the publicly delivered HTML, style.css, app.js, swarm-init.js, and swarm/swarm.js
at https://saai.pro/ on 2026-11-09. The reference snapshots its rendered DOM into a
blueprint and repaints an overlay canvas with cursor agents; scroll advances its
build frontier, and Go live returns to the actual page. Its information structure
uses measured facts, technology tags, layered flagship details, and category
filters. This implementation borrows those interaction ideas with original React
DOM choreography, retaining access to videos and readers during the animation.

Visual browser inspection was unavailable because browser use is disabled in
the app's Work settings. Source analysis must not be described as visual QA.

## Review against the brief

The bold moment is the reconstruction itself. No previous starfield, sculpture,
organic clipping, full-screen introduction, or decorative physics is layered over
it. Counts come from content, tags say what is being studied or practised, and
short questions link to their actual source articles. The visitor can always
choose to read immediately.
